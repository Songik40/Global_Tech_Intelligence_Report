## Verification notes

### NVIDIA / MediaTek
Both companies published the same $3.5B investment and platform-expansion terms. Reuters independently confirms the financing and notes investor concern about ecosystem financing. The report treats strategic benefits as analysis, not guaranteed outcomes.

### LUMI-AI
EuroHPC states a total co-funded budget of €387.8M and expected user availability in 2027. Reuters reports a €387.8M contract with Bull. The report avoids implying that procurement equals delivered compute capacity before installation and acceptance.

### HUMAIN / Applied Intuition
The commitment to deploy thousands of autonomous trucks by 2030 is a company target. The report distinguishes this from vehicles already deployed. Applied Intuition states its SDS is already operating on Level-4 trucks, but no Saudi paid-fleet performance data is yet public.

### BepiColombo
ESA's 31 August briefing confirms the mission status and next operational milestones. The 3 September separation is scheduled, not yet completed as of the report cutoff.

### FSB
The FSB's statement that frontier AI cyber risk is the most immediate AI-related concern to the financial system is an institutional risk assessment. It is not evidence that a systemic cyber event has already occurred.
